Share-token experimental design

This is unaudited example software for local development and public testnets. A token created with it does not establish company ownership, a registry entry, or an investment offer.

Purpose

The contracts demonstrate token creation, allowlisted transfers, and atomic-swap mechanics. Ownership rights and regulatory workflows are outside the example.

Deployment status

This repository does not identify or recommend any live deployment. Deploy to a local network or supported public testnet and keep addresses in your own environment-specific configuration.

Limitations

  • No company, transfer agent, registry, custody, or investor service is supplied.
  • No security audit or production-readiness claim is made.
  • Do not use the example with real assets, funds, or personal information.
