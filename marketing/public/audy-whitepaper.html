AUDY experimental token design

AUDY is unaudited example software for local development and public testnets. It is not issued money, a stablecoin, a financial product, or a promise of redemption.

Purpose

The contracts demonstrate an ERC-20-style token with optional transfer restrictions. They are provided so developers can inspect, test, and adapt the design without relying on a live service.

Deployment status

This repository does not identify or recommend any live deployment. Deploy to a local network or supported public testnet and keep addresses in your own environment-specific configuration.

Limitations

  • No reserves, price peg, redemption mechanism, or issuer is supplied.
  • No security audit or production-readiness claim is made.
  • Do not use the example with real funds or personal information.
