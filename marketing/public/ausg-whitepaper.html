AUSG experimental token design

AUSG is unaudited example software for local development and public testnets. It is not a bond, security, investment product, or claim on any government asset.

Purpose

The example explores token metadata, reference-value updates, and transfer restrictions. It does not provide custody, reserves, yield, liquidity, or ownership rights.

Deployment status

This repository does not identify or recommend any live deployment. Deploy to a local network or supported public testnet and keep addresses in your own environment-specific configuration.

Limitations

  • No issuer, regulated service, return, or redemption right is supplied.
  • No security audit or production-readiness claim is made.
  • Do not use the example with real funds or personal information.
